# How we build a boat

Every boat goes through nine stages. A job number (J-1041) follows the boat
from Order to Delivery. Within a stage there are operations, the individual
pieces of work that people book time to.

## 1. Enquiry
- Take the enquiry (who, what model, when)
- Site visit if a council or commercial customer

## 2. Quote
- Build the quote from the price list
- Send the quote (valid for 30 days)

## 3. Order
- Confirm the order and take the 20% deposit
- Allocate a job number and a hull number
- Order long-lead parts

## 4. Cut & fold
- Cut plate on the CNC
- Fold plate
- Deburr and check

## 5. Weld
- Tack the frame
- Weld the hull
- Leak test
- Grind and finish welds

## 6. Fit-out
- Fit the console and helm seat
- Fit fuel tank and outboard bracket
- Wire electrics and nav lights

## 7. Paint
- Prepare and prime
- Topcoat
- Antifoul

## 8. Sea trial
- Launch
- Run the trial with the customer's skipper where possible
- Write the snag list and fix it

## 9. Delivery
- Final clean
- Delivery checklist
- Handover and invoice

## Notes

- A job can be on only one stage at a time.
- Timesheets are by operation, so we can see where the hours really go.
