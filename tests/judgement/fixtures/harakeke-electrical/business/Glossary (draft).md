# Glossary (draft)

Started by Hana, not finished.

**Customer** - the person or business we do the work for. One record each in
Customers.csv.

**Job** - one piece of work at one address for one customer. It has a job
number: HE- and four digits, for example HE-4129. One number, one job.

**Status** - where a job is up to: Booked, In progress, Waiting on parts,
Done, Invoiced.

**CoC** - Certificate of Compliance. The electrician writes one when the work
is finished and tested. We do not invoice without it.

**Call-out** - an urgent visit, usually a fault. There is a call-out fee on
top of the time and materials.

**Markup** - what we add to the cost of materials. It is a percentage added
to our cost, and it is on the Price List.

**Trade discount** - the discount for the property manager.

**Van stock** - the materials carried in each van. Cable is counted in
metres; everything else is counted each.

**Pay week** - Monday to Sunday.
