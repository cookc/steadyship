# About Harakeke Electrical

Harakeke Electrical is an electrical contractor in Richmond, near Nelson. We
do house wiring and repairs, switchboard upgrades, heat pump and EV charger
circuits, and maintenance for a few schools, shops, orchards and one property
manager around Richmond, Stoke, Mapua and the Motueka valley.

There are ten of us:

- Hana Parata owns the business. She is the master electrician and does the
  quoting, usually on her phone while she is still on site.
- Kiri Walker runs the office: bookings, the Jobs spreadsheet, invoices and
  the pay run.
- Five electricians: Wiremu Tipene, Dylan Fraser, Sione Faleolo, Emma Clarke
  and Tom Bryant.
- Two apprentices: Jack Ngatai and Ruby Scott.
- Brian Cole looks after the stores and the van stock, three mornings a week.

We run three vans (Van 1, Van 2, Van 3).

## How we work with computers

A few of us have made small web pages with Claude for our own bits of the
work. Each one lives in a folder on the shared drive:

- **Job board**: Kiri made it. It is on the TV in the front office, and the
  electricians open it on their phones each morning to see where they are
  going.
- **Invoice maker**: Kiri uses it for every invoice, on the office PC and
  sometimes on her laptop at home.
- **Quick quote**: Hana uses it on site to give a customer a price.
- **Timesheet**: everyone puts their hours in, mostly on the workshop tablet.
  Kiri does the pay run from it on Monday morning.
- **Van stock**: Brian and the electricians use it to see what is in each van
  and what needs reordering.

Our spreadsheets (Jobs, Customers, Materials, the Price List, Brian's van
stocktake) are on the same drive, along with some saved quotes and invoices
(Kiri saves a copy when a customer asks for one). The Jobs spreadsheet is the
one Kiri keeps up to date. Nobody has had time to tidy any of this up.
