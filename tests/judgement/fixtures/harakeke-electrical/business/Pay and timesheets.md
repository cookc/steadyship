# Pay and timesheets

- The pay week runs Monday to Sunday. Kiri does the pay run on Monday morning
  for the week just finished.
- Everyone records their hours each day against the job they were on.
  Workshop time and call-outs with no job number yet are recorded as
  "Workshop" or "Call-out".
- People write their hours either way: 7.5 or 7:30. Both mean seven and a
  half hours.
- Overtime is paid at time and a half for hours over 40 in the week. A long
  day on its own is not overtime.
- Weekend call-outs are part of the same week and count towards the 40.
- Work on a public holiday is paid at time and a half, plus a day in lieu.
- Pay goes into each person's bank account on Wednesday.
