# How a job runs

## 1. Enquiry and quote
- The customer rings or emails. Kiri finds them in Customers.csv, or adds them.
- Small jobs and faults are charged as done. For bigger jobs Hana visits and
  gives a quote from the Price List: labour hours at the hourly rate, and
  materials at what they cost us plus the markup on the Price List.

## 2. Booked
- Kiri gives the job the next job number (HE- and four digits) and adds it to
  the Jobs spreadsheet with the electrician and the date. A job number is
  given once and never used again.
- How to get in (keys, alarm and gate codes) is kept on the customer's record
  in Customers.csv.

## 3. In progress
- The electrician does the work and books their hours to the job.
- If we are held up for a part, the job goes to "Waiting on parts".

## 4. Done
- The electrician tests the work and writes the Certificate of Compliance
  (CoC). Kiri puts the CoC number against the job in the Jobs spreadsheet.

## 5. Invoiced
- No invoice goes out until the CoC number is on the job.
- Labour is charged at the Price List rate for who did the work: electrician,
  apprentice, or after hours.
- Materials are charged at our cost plus the markup.
- The call-out fee is $115 including GST. That is the price on the website
  and on the vans. Everything else on the Price List is before GST.
- The property manager gets the trade discount. It comes off the price before
  GST is added.

## Notes

- A job has one status at a time: Booked, In progress, Waiting on parts, Done
  or Invoiced.
- The Price List says when each price started. Rates last went up on
  1 October 2026.
