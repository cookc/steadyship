# Glossary (draft)

Started by Ana, not finished. Some of this might not match how the
spreadsheets and pages actually work.

**Client** - the person or company we make the job for, written as in
Clients.csv ("Hemi & Kiri Ngata", "Greenstone Homes Ltd"). We say client, not
customer.

**Job** - one piece of work for one client: a kitchen, a set of wardrobes, a
staircase. Always RJ- and four digits, for example RJ-2062. A client can have
more than one job.

**Material code** - the board code in Materials.csv, always ending in the
thickness in mm, for example WMEL-18.

**Operation** - one piece of work inside a stage, such as "Hang doors and set
hinges". Time is booked against the operation, not the whole job.

**Install** - the last stage, when the fitters put the job in on site. Some of
us call it "fit"; the stage is called Install.

**Install date** - the day the fitters start on site. Written year first
(2026-11-13) so the spreadsheets sort it properly.

**Labour rate** - one hourly rate for workshop and install labour. It is in
the Price List.

**Deposit** - 50% of the quote total, paid when the client accepts.

**Quote** - a price from the current Price List. Valid for 30 days.

**Release** - not sure we need this one. Something the builders ask for.
