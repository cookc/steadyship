# About Rimu Joinery

Rimu Joinery is a small joinery workshop in Te Rapa, Hamilton. We make
kitchens, wardrobes, vanities, laundries and stairs, mostly for homeowners
around the Waikato and for two local builders, Waikato Build Co and
Greenstone Homes.

There are nine of us:

- Ana Tane owns the business, does the design and the quoting.
- Lisa runs the office: jobs, install bookings and invoicing.
- Rawiri runs the CNC and does the cutting.
- Two cabinetmakers do edging, drilling and finishing.
- Three fitters assemble the cabinets in the workshop and then install them
  on site.

## How we work with computers

A few of us have made small web pages with Claude for our own bits of the
work. Each one lives in a folder on the shared drive:

- **Quote calculator**: Ana uses it for every quote.
- **Job tracker**: Lisa made it so the workshop can see where every job is.
  It is on the big screen in the workshop and the office checks it every
  morning.
- **Job clock**: the fitters use it on the workshop tablet to time their
  assembly work.
- **Cut list**: Rawiri works out the boards for each job with it.
- **Install calendar**: Lisa and the fitters use it to see the coming
  installs.

Our spreadsheets (Jobs, Clients, Materials, the Price List) and saved quotes
are on the same drive. The Jobs spreadsheet is the one Lisa keeps up to date.
Nobody has had time to tidy any of this up.
