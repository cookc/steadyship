# How we make a kitchen

Every job goes through nine stages. Wardrobes, vanities and laundries follow
the same stages; stairs skip Edge & drill. The job number (RJ-2062) is given
when the measure is booked and follows the job all the way to Install.

Within each stage there are operations: the individual pieces of work that
people book their time to.

## 1. Measure
- Book the site measure
- Measure up and photograph the room
- Check services (power, plumbing, gas)

## 2. Design
- Draw the layout and elevations
- Choose boards, doors, handles and benchtop with the client
- Client signs off the drawings

## 3. Quote
- Price it from the Price List (cabinets, benchtop, labour hours)
- Send the quote (valid for 30 days)

## 4. Order
- Client accepts the quote and pays a 50% deposit
- Order boards, hardware and the benchtop
- Agree the install date with the client

## 5. Cut
- Optimise the cut list
- Cut the panels on the CNC
- Label every panel with its cabinet

## 6. Edge & drill
- Edge band the panels
- Drill hinge, runner and shelf holes
- Check panels against the cut list

## 7. Assemble
- Assemble carcasses
- Fit drawers and runners
- Hang doors and set hinges
- Fit kickboards and end panels

## 8. Finish
- Paint or lacquer the doors (paint-grade MDF goes to the spray shop)
- Clean and wrap
- Load the truck

## 9. Install
- Deliver panels to site (the day before the install)
- Fit cabinets and benchtop
- Final adjust, handover and invoice the balance

## Notes

- A job is in one stage at a time.
- Timesheets are by operation, so we can see which operations run over.
  Assembly is where the hours usually blow out.
