# Glossary (draft)

Started by Morgan, not finished. Some of this might not match how the
spreadsheets and pages actually work.

**Customer** - the company or council we sell to, written exactly as in
Customers.csv ("Harbour Services Ltd"). Never abbreviated.

**Job** - one boat for one customer, from Order to Delivery. Always written
J- and four digits, for example J-1041.

**Model** - the boat design, written in full: "WB 6.2 Workboat", "D 3.6
Dinghy", "P 7.5 Pilot".

**Hull number** - the build serial stamped on the boat. One per job, set at
Order. (We do not write it down anywhere yet.)

**Part** - anything we buy to put in a boat. Identified by the part code in
Parts.csv with hyphens, for example AL-5083-5.

**Operation** - one piece of work inside a stage, such as "Weld the hull".
Time is booked against the operation, not the whole job.

**Quote** - a price for a model plus options from the current price list.
Valid for 30 days.

**Release** - not used here yet. Probably what the office calls it when a job
is passed to the workshop.
