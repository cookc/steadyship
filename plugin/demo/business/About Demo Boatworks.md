# About Demo Boatworks

Demo Boatworks is a small aluminium boat builder in Nelson, New Zealand. We
build workboats, pilot boats and dinghies in our workshop on Wakefield Quay
Road, about 14 people on the floor and four in the office.

Morgan Reid owns the business and does most of the quoting.

## What we build

- WB 6.2 Workboat and WB 7.8 Workboat: general-purpose workboats
- P 7.5 Pilot: pilot boat with a raised wheelhouse
- D 3.6 Dinghy and D 4.2 Dinghy: tenders and small council boats

## Who buys from us

Port and harbour services, charter operators, fishing companies and councils,
mostly in the top of the South Island, Southland and Northland. Most
customers come back for a second boat.

## How we work with computers

A few of us have built small web pages with Claude for our own jobs (a quote
builder, a job board, a timer, a parts list and so on). Each lives in a folder
on the shared drive. Our spreadsheets (jobs, customers, parts, price list)
are on the same drive. Nobody has had time to tidy these up.
